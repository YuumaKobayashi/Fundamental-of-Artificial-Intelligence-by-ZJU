# Words Before Read
When I first entered lessons about computer science in university, I found that I could only use Large Language Model such as Deepseek and Claude but got no idea about Machine Learning, since I have never learnt anything about them during my senior high school.Therefore, I decide to learn it by myself in this summer holiday, in Zhejiang University whose students have produced some great technology this year (2025). And that's why I want to establish my Github Account, and, as you see, this code-warehouse.  
  
But actually, this repository is not about the knowledge of artificial intelligence. As you see, the repository is made up of assignments of a lesson name Fundamental of Artificial Intelligence in Zhejiang University. Well, nobody wants to spend time on it, because of its terrible teaching mode. And that's the real reason I set up this repository, just share my materials, and give an example about the final assignment for who needs to take this class after years.  
  
Taking the advantage of the development of AI, or someone calls it SI for Super Intelligence, students can finish their task in minutes. Maybe they will never find my repository, but for me, it may be the beginning of my journey in computer science.
