# Words Before Read
When I first entered lessons about computer science in university, I found that I could only use large language models such as Deepseek and Claude but had no idea about Machine Learning, since I had never learnt anything about them during my senior high school.Therefore, I decided to learn it by myself during this summer break, at Zhejiang University whose students have produced some great technology this year (2025). And that's why I want to establish my GitHub Account, and, as you see, this repository.  
  
But actually, this repository is not about the knowledge of artificial intelligence. What I mean is that the repository is made up of assignments of a course named Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence in Zhejiang University. Well, nobody wants to spend time on it, because of its terrible teaching style and method, and that's the real reason I set up this repository in the small Starbucks in Chengyue College, Zijingang Campus of Zhejiang University, just to share my materials, and give an example about the final assignment for those who need to take this class in future years.  
  
Taking advantage of the development of AI, or someone calls it SI for Super Intelligence, everyone can finish their tasks in minutes. Maybe they will never find my repository, but for me, it may be the beginning of my journey in computer science.
